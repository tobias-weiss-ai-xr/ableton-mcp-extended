- **Dub Echo FX**: Echoing elements from drop

## 🎶 **AUTHENTIC REGGAE CHARACTERISTICS**

### **1. One Drop Rhythm**
```
Beat: 1     2     3     4
Kick: .     .     .     .
Snare:      X           X
Hats:   X     X     X
Guitar: X     X     X     X (upstrokes on offbeats)
```
The kick **does not** play on beat 1 (the "One Drop")

### **2. Rockers Rhythm**
```
Beat: 1     2     3     4
Kick: .     X     .     X
Snare:      X           X
Hats:   X     X     X
```
Kick plays on 2 and 4 (cross-stick feel)

### **3. Steppers Rhythm**
```
Beat: 1  2  3  4
Kick: X  .  X  .
Snare:    X     X
Hats:  X  X  X  X
```
Kick on 1 and 3 (four-on-the-floor for reggae)

## 🎚️ **PRODUCTION TIPS**

### **Bass**
- **Keep it simple**: Root and fifth, occasional minor seventh
- **Sub content**: Mix a sine wave (below 50Hz) with the bass
- **Sidechain**: Sidechain bass to kick for cleaner low end
- **EQ**: Boost 80-100Hz, cut 200-300Hz

### **Guitar**
- **The Skank**: Play on offbeats (between beats)
- **Permutation**: `|| X-|| X-||` (bar 1 and 3) or `|| X-|| X-|| X-|| X-||` (every bar)
- **Muting**: Sharp palm muting for biting sound
- **Chords**: Use triads, suggest F major, C major (no thirds for roots feel)

### **Keyboards/Organ**
- **Leslie**: Use Leslie rotary effect for authentic sound
- **Chords**: Triads, root position, riffs over chord changes
- **Syncopation**: Play on offbeats, create motion

### **Drums**
- **One Drop**: No kick on beat 1, emphasis on 2 and 4
- **Snare**: Use a tight, dry snare with some room
- **Hi-Hats**: Play on offbeats (eighths or sixteenths)
- **Percussion**: Add timbales, guiro, or percussion loops

### **Dub Effects**
- **Echo**: 1/4 or 1/8 note delay, 60-80% feedback
- **Reverb**: Spring reverb on drums, room reverb on others
- **Send to Echo**: Does not need to be precise; create echo returns

## 🎚️ **MIXING RECOMMENDATIONS**

### **Low End**
- Keep bass and kick separate in frequency
- Bass: 40-100Hz (main), 1-2kHz (string noise)
- Kick: 60-100Hz (thud), 2-4kHz (click)

### **Mids**
- Guitar and organ occupy mids but on different frequencies
- Plant guitar around 800Hz-2kHz
- Plant organ around 200-800Hz

### **Highs**
- Hi-hats: 6-12kHz
- Snare: 5-10kHz
- Organ high end: 3-8kHz

### **Dub Processing**
- Use bus sends for echo and reverb (not inserts)
- Echo feedback: 60-80% for authentic dub
- Reverb: 2-4 seconds on drums, shorter on instruments
